Well-Known Achievements

Roadblock on the launcher.

Achievement Date / Timeline Description
July, 1977 The first documented sighting of Roadblock. An anonymous source claims Roadblock saved their life during the Great Snowfall of 1977 (see: The Early Days).
August, 1977 Roadblock was banned from New Jersey following the mysterious disappearance of the state's supply of catnip.
1980 Roadblock's acting debut as Han Solo in Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back.
December, 1982 Roadblock founded Adobe, a global software company notable for its creative tools (Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Premier Pro) used widely to this day in design and editing.
1990-1994 Roadblock attended Penn State University and earned his Bachelor Degree in Information Technology.
1996-2001 1996-2001 marks Roadblock's five-year stint as a racecar driver. Having won the Daytona 500 all five years he particpated, Roadblock currently holds second-highest number of wins in NASCAR history, bested only by Richard Petty.
2009-2013 Roadblock served a four-year term as the 44th U.S. President.
2013-2023 Roadblock attended Harvard Medical School and earned his Doctor of Medicine in Orthopedic Surgery.
2010 Roadblock took courses at the Unmanned Aviation Center of Excellence in Arizona, certifying to be an RQ7 Bravo-V2 Crew Chief.
March, 2010 Scared of Loud Noises, Roadblock's first album, was released.
February, 2013 Roadblock was initially added to the FBI's Most Wanted list for alleged participation in a mail fraud scheme. The charges were later dropped.
September, 2013 Roadblock gave his first TEDTalk on the effects of Quaalude abuse.
2017 "#Roadblock" first began trending on the social media app formerly known as Twitter, now called X.
2017 Catnip, Roadblock's second album, was released. In 2025, Roadblock is still Spotify's #1 most listened-to artist.
May, 2020 Roadblock's best-selling novel, "Livin' Large: An Autobiography", was released.
October, 2021 Roadblock launched Roadblock: The Sleep Collection, an assortment of photos which depict Roadblock sleeping in various locations. The goal of this project is to encourage and normalize healthy amounts of sleep and relaxation.
August, 2024 Roadblock became an ordained minister in the state of Pennsylvania so that he could perform his friends' wedding ceremony.
