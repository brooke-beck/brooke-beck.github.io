Current Endeavors

These days, Roadblock can most often be found relaxing in the comfort of his Pennsylvania home. Unconfirmed rumors speculate that Roadblock might be working on another tech project. These rumors are the result of an image posted to Reddit with the caption, "Blocc on Puter". Once the post began to garner attention, it was removed - but the similarity between the individual in the photo and Roadblock is uncanny. The image appears to depict Roadblock himself sat in front of a keyboard, monitor, and mouse (!). Upon zooming into the photo, some viewers claim to be able to see computer code typed on the screen. Roadblock has not commented on this.

Other recent updates from Roadblock indicate that he trying to enjoy the quiet life. One X post from his account reads, "I'm not ignoring you, I'm just prioritizing me. #BlockedByRoadblock". Although we cannot confirm at this time who this post was directed at, some believe it was indirect commentary surrounding cell phones, social media, and texting - the way these things have shaped our interactions with each other. The constant connection that technology provides can be a detriment to those of us who prefer to be left alone most of the time.

Aside from breaking the Internet, Roadblock has confirmed that he is planning on releasing his next album in 2027. In a June 2025 interview on Good Morning America, Roadblock stated that he has been exploring new genres as of late - namely, R&B and Tuvan throat singing. Fans eagerly await the drop of his next album.
